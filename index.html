The Biggest Mistakes Business Owners Make

Avoid these and you'll save months of time and money.

❌

Spending on ads before having a proper website

Why it happensAds feel faster than building a website.
What to do insteadGet a simple, clear website or landing page first — then run ads to it.
❌

Posting randomly on Instagram

Why it happensIt feels productive to post something every day.
What to do insteadPost with a purpose — offers, proof, and clear ways to contact you.
❌

Ignoring Google Reviews

Why it happensReviews seem secondary compared to ads and posts.
What to do insteadAsk happy customers for reviews. They build trust faster than most ads.
❌

Trying every marketing strategy at once

Why it happensFear of missing out — everyone sells a different “must-have”.
What to do insteadPick 1–2 channels that fit your budget and do them well.
❌

Hiring the cheapest freelancer

Why it happensMarketing feels expensive, so the lowest quote wins.
What to do insteadPay for clarity and results — cheap work often costs more later.
❌

Expecting results in one week

Why it happensAds and agencies promise overnight success.
What to do insteadGive a channel 30–90 days with clear tracking before judging it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Simple answers for business owners who are still figuring things out.

Do I need a website?

If customers ask for your details, compare options, or find you on Google — yes, even a simple website helps. If you're brand new and still testing your offer, Instagram + WhatsApp can work for a short while.

Should I run Meta Ads?

Only when you have a clear offer and a place to send people (website or landing page). Running ads too early usually wastes budget.

Is SEO worth it?

Yes — if you can wait. SEO is slower than ads but can bring free customers for years. Best when you're also doing other marketing, not as your only move on day one.

How much should I spend?

Start with what you can sustain for 3 months. ₹0–₹5,000 builds foundations. ₹5,000–₹20,000 starts generating enquiries. ₹20,000+ can scale faster with ads and systems.

Can I do marketing myself?

Yes for basics — Google Business Profile, simple Instagram posts, asking for reviews. Once ads and tracking get involved, guidance usually saves money.

How long before I see results?

Ads can show signals in 1–4 weeks. SEO takes months. Branding and websites pay off when people start trusting and contacting you — often immediately once they're live.
