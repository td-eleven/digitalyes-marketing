Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers to the questions business owners actually ask.

Do I really need a website?

If customers look you up online, yes — even a simple website helps. Without one, many people may trust a competitor who looks more professional. If you're still testing your offer, Instagram and WhatsApp can work for a short while, but a website usually becomes necessary soon.

Should I choose Facebook Ads or Google Ads?

Facebook Ads are useful when you want to reach people quickly with an offer. Google Ads are better when people already search for your service. If you're unsure, message me with your business type — I'll recommend one.

How much should I spend every month?

For ads, I usually recommend around ₹10,000/month in ad budget on top of the ₹10,000 service fee for a 2-month campaign. That gives the campaign enough room to learn. A website is a one-time ₹10,000 investment.

Can I run ads without a website?

You can, but it's usually not ideal. People need a clear place to land and contact you. Both lead generation packages include landing page creation if you don't have a website yet.

How quickly can I expect enquiries?

With Facebook or Google Ads, businesses often begin seeing enquiries within around 48 hours after campaigns go live. Results vary by industry, competition, audience, and budget. Websites are typically ready in 7–10 days.

Do you work with businesses outside Bangalore?

Yes. Most of the work is online — WhatsApp, calls, and shared drafts — so location is rarely a problem.

How long does website development take?

Usually 7–10 days once we have your content and details. More complex sites can take a little longer.
