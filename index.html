FAQ

Straight answers about our three services.

Do I need a website before running ads?

Usually yes. Ads work better when people land on a clear page. Both lead generation packages include landing page creation if you don't have one yet.

Is the ₹10,000 fee the total cost for ads?

No. ₹10,000 is our fixed service fee for 2 months. Your ad spend (recommended ~₹10,000/month) is paid separately to Meta or Google.

Meta Ads or Google Ads — which should I choose?

Google Ads if people already search for your service. Meta Ads if you want to reach local audiences with an offer. Unsure? Message us — we'll recommend one.

What's included in website yearly support?

₹3,000/year covers basic maintenance and after-launch support so small updates and issues don't leave you stuck.

How long before I see enquiries from ads?

Meta often shows early signals in 2–4 weeks. Google Ads can show intent sooner (1–2 weeks). Full learning usually takes the 2-month campaign window.

Can I start with just a website?

Yes. Many businesses start with a website at ₹8,000+, then add Meta or Google lead generation once they're ready to invest in ads.
