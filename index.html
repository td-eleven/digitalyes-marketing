Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers to the questions business owners actually ask.

Will this work for my business?

Most service and local businesses can benefit from the right mix of a website, Facebook Ads, or Google Ads — but not every business needs all three. That's why we start with your business type, goals, and budget before recommending anything. If the timing isn't right, we'll tell you that too.

Do I really need a website?

If customers look you up online, yes — even a simple website helps. Without one, many people may trust a competitor who looks more professional. If you're still testing your offer, Instagram and WhatsApp can work for a short while, but a website usually becomes necessary soon.

Should I choose Facebook Ads or Google Ads?

Facebook Ads are useful when you want to reach people quickly with an offer. Google Ads are better when people already search for your service. If you're unsure, message me with your business type — I'll recommend one.

How much should I spend on advertising?

For most small businesses starting ads, I recommend around ₹10,000/month in ad budget on top of the ₹10,000 service fee for a 2-month campaign. That gives the ads enough room to learn what works. Spending much less often means slower learning and weaker results. If your budget is tighter, we can talk about what to do first — and what can wait. A website is a separate one-time ₹10,000 investment.

Why shouldn't I run Facebook Ads without a website?

You can run ads without a full website, but people still need a clear place to land and contact you. Without that, many click, get confused, and leave. That's why both lead generation packages include a landing page — so your ads have somewhere useful to send people.

How quickly can I expect leads or phone calls?

With Facebook or Google Ads, leads usually start coming in within 1–2 business days after campaigns go live. Results vary by industry, competition, audience, and budget. Websites are typically ready in 7–10 days.

Do you work with businesses outside Bangalore?

Yes. Most of the work is online — WhatsApp, calls, and shared drafts — so location is rarely a problem.

How long does website development take?

Usually 7–10 days once we have your content and details. More complex sites can take a little longer.
